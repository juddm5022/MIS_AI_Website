Meet Our Group

We are a group of five college students learning about Management Information Systems (MIS) and AI ethics. Our group members are Mckynzie Judd, Coltyn Judd, Addison Rodriguez, Brayson Beck, and John Kvistad. We made this website to share what we learned about AI, malware, hackers, and cybersecurity. We hope our website helps people understand these topics better.

Why We Chose These Topics

We chose these topics because technology is a big part of our everyday lives. AI is becoming more popular, so we think it is important to know how to use it correctly. We also wanted to learn about hackers and malware because they can put people's information at risk. These topics can help us stay safe online and make better choices when using technology.

What We Learned

While making this website, we learned more about how technology is used in everyday life. We learned that AI can be helpful, but it needs to be used the right way. We also learned about different types of malware and how hackers can steal information. This project helped us understand why cybersecurity is important.
