What Are Malware and Hackers?

Malware is harmful software that can damage computers or steal information. Hackers are people who try to get into computer systems. Some hackers help find security problems, while others break into systems without permission. Malware and hackers can cause problems for businesses and people.

Malware and Cyber Threats

The WannaCry Ransomware Attack

On May 12, 2017, a malware attack called WannaCry spread to computers around the world. It locked people's files and demanded money to unlock them. The attack affected hospitals, businesses, and other organizations. This event showed why keeping computers updated is important for cybersecurity.

Types of Malware

Here are three common types of malware:

  • Viruses: Harmful programs that can spread to other files and damage computers.
  • Ransomware: Malware that locks your files and demands money to unlock them.
  • Spyware: Software that secretly collects information from your computer.

Common Hacker Motives

  1. Stealing money or personal information.
  2. Breaking into computer systems.

Ways to Stay Safe Online

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Do not click on suspicious links.
  • Keep your computer updated.

What Is a Network Packet?

A network packet is a small piece of data sent from one computer to another. Packets help information travel across the internet. Hackers may try to steal information from packets that are not protected.

How to Stay Safe from Hackers

There are simple ways to protect yourself from hackers and malware. People should use strong passwords and avoid clicking on strange links. It is also important to keep computers and phones updated. These steps can help keep personal information safe.

Ways to Protect Yourself

  1. Use strong passwords.
  2. Do not click on suspicious links.
  3. Keep your devices updated.
  4. Turn on two-factor authentication.
