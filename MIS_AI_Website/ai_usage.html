Correct AI Usage

Our Thoughts on Correct AI Usage

We think using AI in college is okay as long as you use it the right way. AI can help students understand things they are confused about in class. It can also help with studying for tests and explaining homework. Students should not use AI to cheat or do all their assignments for them. We think AI is a good tool because it can make learning easier. As long as students still do their own work and follow their professor's rules, AI can be helpful in college.

AI Feedback on Our Thoughts

ChatGPT agrees that AI can be helpful for college students when used responsibly. Using AI to study, understand homework, and learn difficult topics are good examples. It is also important that students do their own assignments and follow their professor's rules. One thing to remember is that AI can sometimes give incorrect information, so students should always double-check its answers.

Incorrect AI Usage

Our Thoughts on Incorrect AI Usage

We think using AI the wrong way is when students use it to cheat on their schoolwork. Students should not copy AI answers and turn them in as their own. This is not fair to students who do their own work. AI should help students learn, not do all the work for them.

Incorrect Ways to Use AI

Cheating
Using AI to get answers on a test.

Copying Work
Turning in AI answers as your own work.

Not Checking Answers
Trusting AI without checking if it is correct.
